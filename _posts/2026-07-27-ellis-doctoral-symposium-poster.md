I presented a poster on our FWF project [*Information Planes and Decompositions*](https://www.fwf.ac.at/en/research-radar/10.55776/PAT7753623) at the [ELLIS Doctoral Symposium 2026](https://sites.google.com/view/eds2026lisbon/home) in Lisbon. The symposium brings together ELLIS PhD students and faculty from across Europe, which made it a great place to get technical feedback on our work and to meet people in the international machine learning community.
