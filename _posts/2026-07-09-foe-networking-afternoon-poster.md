---

Together with [Nikolaus Kopp](https://tugraz.elsevierpure.com/en/persons/nikolaus-kopp/), I presented a poster on our FWF project [*Information Planes and Decompositions*](https://www.fwf.ac.at/en/research-radar/10.55776/PAT7753623) at the Networking Afternoon of TU Graz's Field of Expertise *Information, Communication & Computing*. With around 500 participants and nearly 100 posters, the event brought together students, doctoral candidates and faculty from many disciplines. Our poster led to good discussions and to possible collaborations with researchers in medicine, biology, geodesy and machine learning.
