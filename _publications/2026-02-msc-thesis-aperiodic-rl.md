excerpt: "My M.Sc. thesis extends my B.Sc. work from the Penrose P3 tiling to Penrose P2 and the recently discovered aperiodic monotile, packaged as reinforcement-learning environments in the gymnasium framework (`Penrose-P3-v0`, `Penrose-P2-v0`, `Einstein-v0`). It also develops an algorithm that exploits novelty in Markov chains to improve navigation. Whereas periodic environments repeat the same structure everywhere, aperiodic tilings have regularities that depend on scale: increasing the agent's radius of exploration demands more structural information and makes convergence explicitly scale-dependent. The thesis is not publicly available."
